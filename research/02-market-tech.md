# Darshify market and technology research (v2: immersive Spotify fidelity + name SEO/AEO/GEO)

**Research date:** 17 October 2026 (Agent 2, Market & Tech)
**Supersedes:** the 29 Aug 2026 "recruiter-first" version, which is still in git history (`git show 60fd7a2:research/02-market-tech.md`).
**Product under study:** Darshify, the personal portfolio of **Darshil Jain** (BBA Business & Industry, Maharaja Surajmal Institute (MSI), GGSIPU, Delhi). It is built with React 19, React Router 7 framework mode with static prerender on Vercel, Vite 8, Tailwind 4, Motion 13 and TypeScript, and is live at `https://wrapped-portfolio.vercel.app`.
**New goals:** (a) the site should feel close to indistinguishable from the real Spotify app and leave visitors "mesmerized"; (b) searches for "Darshil Jain" or "Darshil" on Google, Bing and AI assistants should surface Darshil and this site first.

## Executive summary

1. **Nobody currently owns the "Darshil Jain" query, and this site is invisible.** Results for "Darshil Jain" go to other people: LinkedIn profiles of a Bhavik Steel Industries export/marketing manager, plus ZoomInfo listings for Darshil Jains at Viacom18, CoreWeave, Microland, RRA and Pararth Products. No result mentions this Darshil, his LinkedIn slug, Igniters Club or `wrapped-portfolio.vercel.app` (§4). The opportunity is real, but he needs **disambiguating qualifiers** (BBA · MSI Delhi · GGSIPU · operations & strategy · Igniters Club founder).
2. **Get a custom domain now.** `darshiljain.com` returns _No match_ in WHOIS (it looks unregistered as of 17 Oct 2026). `darshiljain.in` is registered and parked at Hostinger, owner unknown. `vercel.app` is on the Public Suffix List, so the current host is not penalized, but it is neither a brand nor portable (§3.1).
3. **The current HTML is good, but the entity layer is thin.** Pages are prerendered with real titles, canonicals, H1s and `<a href>`s, and unknown URLs return a real 404. However, the **home page has no JSON-LD**, the Person schema lives only on `/artist`, its `image` is an **SVG placeholder**, `sameAs` lists only LinkedIn, there is no `WebSite`/`ProfilePage`/`alumniOf`/`alternateName`, case-study titles omit his name, the sitemap has no `lastmod`, and there is no real photo anywhere (§4.2).
4. **For AI assistants the levers are retrieval, not special files.** Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode need _no_ special files or schema. Evidence shows no measurable citation effect from `llms.txt`. Leading AI crawlers do not execute JavaScript, so content must be in the initial HTML, which Darshify already does. The highest-leverage moves are: be indexed in **Google, Bing and Brave**; allow AI search bots; publish an answer-first, fact-dense "Who is Darshil Jain?" block; and keep the entity data consistent across LinkedIn, Unstop and the site (§3.2–3.3).
5. **Copy Spotify's grammar, not its marks.** The live web player (Oct 2026) uses proprietary **SpotifyMixUI** fonts and Encore tokens (`#121212` base, `#1ed760` accent). Recreate the layout, interactions and color behavior. Never ship the Spotify logo, wordmark or Spotify Mix/Circular fonts. Use **Figtree** (OFL, about 20 KB latin variable WOFF2) as the UI font (§1.1, §6).
6. **Library picks:** **Base UI** (`@base-ui/react` 1.9.0, stable since Dec 2025, has a stable Drawer, Context Menu, Menu, Slider, Tooltip and Dialog) replaces Radix + vaul. **vaul is officially unmaintained.** Use **react-resizable-panels** v4 for the resizable sidebars, **build-time color extraction with `sharp.stats().dominant`** (already a dependency, zero runtime), and native `<audio>` + Media Session. Avoid **howler** (last release Sep 2023). Keep Motion as the only animation runtime; 14.x is out and is non-breaking for the public API (§2).

- **No competitor combines high fidelity with crawlable, entity-rich HTML.** The two most feature-rich (Joshify, Franco) are empty shells to AI crawlers. A _mesmerize_ moment for Darshil could be a short **real** audio intro or "artist's note" voiced by Darshil, with synced captions acting as lyrics (inference; it must be opt-in, see §6).

## 3. Best-practice patterns

### 3.1 Personal-name SEO in 2026

**Domain**

- `vercel.app` is on the Public Suffix List (`// Vercel, Inc` section lists `vercel.app`, `vercel.dev`, `now.sh`) ([PSL](https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat)), so search engines treat `wrapped-portfolio.vercel.app` as its own site. There is no shared-subdomain penalty, but the name ("wrapped-portfolio") carries neither his name nor a brand and cannot move with him (inference).
- WHOIS on 17 Oct 2026: **`darshiljain.com` → "No match"** (appears unregistered). `darshify.com` → "No match". `darshiljain.in` → registered and parked on Hostinger DNS (`ns1.dns-parking.com`), owner unknown; check whether Darshil owns it. `darshil.in` → already in use (Netlify DNS). (Direct `whois`/`dig` lookups.)
- **Recommendation:** register `darshiljain.com` (optionally also `darshify.com` and redirect it). Make it the Vercel production domain and 301/308-redirect `wrapped-portfolio.vercel.app` → `darshiljain.com` ([Vercel: deploying & redirecting domains](https://vercel.com/docs/domains/working-with-domains/deploying-and-redirecting)). Update every canonical, `og:url`, `@id` and the sitemap at the same time. ### 3.3 GEO (generative-engine optimization)

- **No special files are needed for Google AI features:** "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup", and "There's also no special schema.org structured data that you need to add" ([Google: AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), updated 10 Dec 2025).
- **`llms.txt` evidence:** SE Ranking's ~300k-domain analysis found no statistical link between having the file and AI citations. Ahrefs found 97% of 137k domains with the file got zero requests for it in May 2026. Google's John Mueller compared it to the meta keywords tag. No major AI vendor has confirmed reading it ([nytroseo summary](https://nytroseo.com/does-llms-txt-help-2026-evidence), [guptadeepak.com](https://guptadeepak.com/llms-txt-meta-keywords-2026/), [geoly.ai](https://www.geoly.ai/blog/does-llms-txt-work); secondary sources). **Verdict:** optional. Generate `/llms.txt` from the same typed data at build time (it is cheap and can help agentic browsing), but do not count it as a ranking lever. The live site currently 404s on `/llms.txt`.
- **Content that gets cited:** the KDD 2024 GEO paper found **Quotation Addition (+~42%), Statistics Addition (+~33%), Fluency (+~29%) and Cite Sources (+~28%)** improved visibility in generative answers, while **Keyword Stuffing was ~9% worse than baseline**. Gains were far larger for _lower-ranked_ sources (for example Cite Sources +115% at rank 5) ([Aggarwal et al., arXiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)). ### 3.4 Making an app-like site fully crawlable

- AI crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot) **do not render JavaScript**. Gemini (via Googlebot) and AppleBot do. Vercel's recommendation: render "main content, metadata, navigation" on the server ([Vercel, Dec 2024](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler)).
- Google: links are discoverable only as `<a href>`. Use the History API, not fragments. Give each route a unique title and description, return real 404s or `noindex` on error views, and keep in mind that Google still recommends server-side or pre-rendering because not every bot runs JavaScript ([Google: JS SEO basics](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics), updated 4 Mar 2026).
- React Router prerendering writes `[url].html` plus `[url].data` for each listed path. With `ssr: true`, unlisted paths are server-rendered ([React Router: pre-rendering](https://reactrouter.com/how-to/pre-rendering)). Darshify already prerenders `canonicalRouteInventory` (`react-router.config.ts`).
- **Fidelity rules that keep content crawlable** (inference from the sources above):
  - The Library list, the Now Playing "About" card and every playlist's rows must be in the SSR HTML, not mounted after interaction.
  - Collapsed panes hide content with CSS or `inert`, not by unmounting it.
  - The right-click context menu only _adds_ shortcuts to destinations that already exist as links.
  - Keep one `<h1>` per route and semantic `<h2>`s for shelves ("Recruiter Essentials", "Evidence collections").

## 7. Conflicts for synthesis (surface in Phase 2, do not resolve silently)

1. **Fidelity vs. identity.** The previous research (29 Aug 2026) argued that "a closer Spotify clone cannot" support a 10/10 portfolio and recommended an original visual system. The user now wants a near-indistinguishable Spotify experience. **Proposed resolution:** match layout, interactions, shortcuts, color _behavior_ and density 1:1. Keep the brand layer original: logo, font, name and a slightly shifted accent green (for example not exactly `#1ed760`). Add the non-affiliation disclosure.
2. **The name "Darshify".** Spotify's developer branding rules say an app name must not "sound or look similar" to Spotify ([Spotify Design Guidelines](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design)). Those rules govern apps on Spotify's platform, which Darshify is not. The risk for a non-commercial personal portfolio is low, but not zero (inference, not legal advice). For SEO, the **person's name**, not "Darshify", is the asset. Recommendation: lead with "Darshil Jain" in titles and domain, and keep "Darshify" as the in-app brand. The user decides.
3. **Recruiter-first vs. mesmerize.** The previous plan front-loaded résumé, email and LinkedIn CTAs. The new goal is immersion. **Proposed resolution:** keep the immersion opt-in (full-screen and audio intro), but keep the answer-first facts and contact links in the SSR HTML and visible within the first viewport, because both AEO and recruiters need them.
